Commercial title, explained

The questions investors ask us most.

What property type requires title insurance?

Although title insurance is common across all real estate transactions, its significance escalates in commercial dealings due to the typically elevated purchase prices and intricate purchase agreements involved.

Why do I need commercial title insurance?

It safeguards the investor if complications arise concerning the property's title — from negligence, clerical errors, or even fraud. Without a policy, the investor risks losing their investment.

What policies exist, and who pays?

There are two: the lender's policy and the owner's policy. If the buyer uses a lender, they purchase both. The owner's policy provides coverage for as long as they own the property.

How do you acquire title insurance?
