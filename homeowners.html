The basics

What is title insurance — and why it matters.

Unlike other insurance that protects against future events, title insurance protects you against problems from the past. A one-time premium at closing covers you for as long as you own your home.

Owner's policy

Protects your equity and ownership rights against covered title defects — for as long as you or your heirs own the property.

Lender's policy

Required by most mortgage lenders to protect their interest in the property — often issued alongside your owner's policy.

One-time cost

You pay a single premium at closing — no monthly bills, no renewals. Lasting protection for one of your largest investments.
